FAQ

Straight answers.

How is the site survey arranged?

After first contact we book a confirmed appointment. Our technical team visits the site, takes measurements and assesses the right product type.

How quickly will I get a quote?

A detailed quote is sent within 48 hours of the site survey and remains valid for 30 days from issue.

uPVC or aluminium — what’s the difference?

uPVC offers excellent thermal insulation at a lower cost and is ideal for homes. Thermal aluminium allows slimmer profiles, larger openings and RAL colours — preferred for facades, sliding doors and commercial buildings. We help you choose based on the building, budget and design.

How do payments work?

For supply, typically a 50% deposit on signing and 50% before delivery. For supply with installation, 50% deposit and 50% after handover. All payments are made by bank transfer to the company account, as set out in the contract.

Do you install products bought elsewhere?

Yes. We also offer installation, removal or sealing as a standalone service when the client supplies the product.

Do you work outside Tirana or abroad?

Yes. We deliver and install across Albania, and prepare quotes for international clients in English or Italian, priced in EUR.
